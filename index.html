Field Nav

In-cab swath Guidance for sprayers, spreaders, and mowers. Mark a row, set the boom, and stay off the last pass.

  • Lightbar, parallel passes, and a painted coverage map
  • Phone GPS, compass, gyro, and motion used together. Works without cell coverage
  • Optional beeps so you can watch the boom, not the phone

On iPhone, open this page in Safari. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. When you start Guidance, allow Location and Motion & Orientation.
